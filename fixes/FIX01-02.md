**Date:** 11 October 2026.

**Status:** Phase 0 implemented and verified offline. Phase 1 executed all 12
approved trials but failed its semantic selection gate (baseline 1/6 correct,

Phase 4 offline recovery integration are implemented. The subsequently

The separately approved 60-call isolation comparison also completed and failed

The inherited diagnostic gates passed: admission/correct attribution improved

batching/isolation remain unqualified; Phase 4 live approval remains unused and

Offline implementation does not itself approve
diagnostic comparisons or amend accepted production authority.

**Scope:** Address the remaining semantic attribution failure after
[FIX01-01](FIX01-01.md), then demonstrate that correct attribution enables useful
repair and publication. Incorporate the relevant independent assessment routing
from [FIX02](FIX02.md) without repeating its implementation or assuming that a
different model setting is the solution. [FIX01](FIX01.md) remains the backlog.

## 1. Recommendation and intended outcome

First prove that the latest defective story is repairable under the existing
permission contract when supplied a correct diagnosis. Then isolate the effect
of the loss request's supporting context on semantic judgments. Test model effort
as a separate factor if needed. Implement the smallest shared change supported
by that evidence, and require actual repair, publication and rubric acceptance.

The desired observable chain is:

> Detect a real output defect → identify its actual owner → authorize only the
> permitted change → repair the missing meaning → revalidate → publish useful output.

FIX01-01 improved the mechanics of this chain. It did not establish correct
semantic judgments or improved published output. Independent assessment settings
can make model selection controllable; they cannot themselves establish either.

Keep three completion claims separate:

| Claim | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanics implemented | Shared packet, admission, attribution, authorization and lifecycle tests pass. |
| Semantic improvement demonstrated | Controlled live comparisons improve meaning judgments without new false ownership or missed losses. |
| Recovery/output goal achieved | The production workflow performs an appropriate repair when needed and publishes output meeting the case's rubric. |

No finite test establishes reliability across every model. The engine must handle
variation through explicit contracts, preserved evidence and safe failure; it
must not accept an incorrect interpretation merely to continue.

**Feasibility conclusion:** the first recovery proof and a shared presentation
candidate fit existing owners and repair permissions. There is no identified
architecture blocker for those steps. Semantic benefit remains unproven. The
pre-dispatch calibration work, conditional assessment-routing decision and explicit
publication-quality gate below are prerequisites, not tasks that can be skipped
because the current JSON contract already passes.

## 3. Architectural findings and remaining brittleness

### 3.1 Proven limitations

- **Admission proves evidence structure, not semantic truth.**
  [Comparison admission](../src/domain/source_omission_comparison.zig) checks
  complete assignments, currentness, selected members, source spans and
  explanations. A valid coordinate can point to evidence that contradicts the
  model's explanation. More native joins cannot prove that explanation correct.
- **Several representations repeat in the same loss packet.**
  [Packet construction](../src/domain/specification_support.zig) sends the fixed
  finding, sources, overlapping member collections, the deficient subject with
  surrounding candidate context, and reconstructed producer evidence.
  [Supporting projection](../src/domain/source_omission_context.zig) repeats
  requirements through extraction, dispositions and signals. Comparison category
  removal did not remove all this repetition.
- **Assigned meaning and visible context are intentionally different.**
  [Authoring](../src/domain/specification_session.zig) receives original bound
  requirements, other contextual requirements, sources and eligible exact-copy
  choices. [Loss binding](../src/domain/source_omission_binding.zig) resolves
  actual role membership. Adding every visible token or contextual requirement
  to that membership would conceal genuine role omissions.
- **A literal's presence is not its meaningful use.**
  [Exact coverage](../src/domain/specification_coverage.zig) sees the story's
  existing exact reference. It cannot prove the narrative says to display it.
  The strings `kind` and `exact_copy` are legal inert prose, not typed references
  that the parser should reinterpret or universally ban.
- **A wrong finding can contaminate otherwise consistent localization.**
  The loss task retains its admitted finding; it cannot reassess whether the
  original omission was real. Detection accuracy and attribution accuracy need
  separate measurements. Call 15 is credible here, but this cannot be assumed
  for all future cases.
- **The final rejection loses useful causal detail.**
  `AuthorizationRejection` retains an aggregate review origin and outstanding
  requirements; [diagnostic origin selection](../src/domain/candidate_validation_diagnostic.zig)
  returns no response origin for omission authorization. The report's
  `candidate_model_call=null` does not expose calls 9, 15 and 16 directly, although
  their evidence survives deeper in the record. The null single-call association
  is intentional for an assembled-candidate rejection; the missing convenience
  is separate per-finding causal evidence, not a mandatory single response origin.

### 3.2 Hypotheses to test, not explanations to assert

| Hypothesis | Controlled test | What would not establish it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supporting context draws attention away from the assigned collections. | Change only redundant/supporting presentation, preserving all governing facts and comparison memberships. | A smaller token count or one successful retry. |
| The model equates a missing member/representation with missing meaning. | Embedded-value, paraphrase, split/joint-support and genuine missing-condition cases; optional order/ID perturbations as a separate diagnostic. | Repeating the same greeting with different names alone. |
| Assessment effort changes judgment quality. | Same captured body and native contract at separately supported efforts; preserve allowance, temperature and response mode. | Changing generation and assessment together, or assuming high is better. |
| Fragment construction causes recurring authoring defects. | Evaluate composition and supported meaning separately across literal, no-literal and distinct-occurrence sources. | Valid JSON, an exact token somewhere, or a fluent story with invented sequencing. |

The previous [composition example experiment](../test/calibration/authoring-guidance/composition-example/README.md)
improved literal composition from 0/6 to 6/6 but introduced unsupported sequencing
and failed its no-regression gate. Do not promote that candidate as an already
validated remedy, or repeat another prompt example without measuring the same risk.

### 3.5 Additional research and its application

CheckList distinguishes basic behavior checks, meaning-preserving variations and
changes expected to alter a judgment. That supports testing this diagnosis with
both preserved paraphrases and deliberately removed conditions, rather than only
counting overall successes. This is a methodology reference, not evidence that a
particular prompt or model will work here.
[Ribeiro et al., ACL 2020, §2.2](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442.pdf).

NIST's Generative AI Profile recommends empirical assessment under deployment-like
conditions and documenting limits of generalization; it warns against capability
claims from narrow anecdotal assessments. Applied here, captured-call comparisons
support bounded diagnosis claims, while live publication and source-based output
review remain separate requirements.
[NIST AI 600-1, MEASURE 2.3 and 2.5](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf).

Repository-specific consequences: freeze expected meanings independently of model
responses; report per-case/behavior results and repeated-trial variation; keep
fresh qualification cases out of prompt tuning; and test known losses as well as
equivalent representations. Use existing harnesses. No research framework,
additional runtime judge or production dependency is required.
