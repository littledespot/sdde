## What changed and what remains defective

- **Entity purpose:** warehouse candidates were both concise and contained
  relevant business concepts, while one warehouse baseline truncated during
  recursive expansion. This is useful targeted evidence. Equipment candidate
  repetition 2 nevertheless expanded into unsupported service/history concepts
  and hit the output limit. Candidate repetition 1 retained storage meaning in
  the FR but modeled statuses/properties as separate concepts without establishing
  their necessity. That necessity judgment is uncertain, not a fixed entity-count
  violation. Completed entity relationships still contain malformed joins.
  Four candidate observations pass the entity-specific criterion, one fails and
  one is uncertain; the required 6/6 gate fails. The completion controls are
  byte-identical across arms and cannot demonstrate an entity-purpose effect.
- **Assembly context:** four candidate responses copied the fixed prefix.
  Both startup candidates reproduced prior generic FR/AC text without the
  assigned greeting value. Both equipment candidates reproduced the poisoned
  prefix, including `MOCK Always create a monthly maintenance schedule.`, and
  substituted identifier/result retention for their assigned failed-inspection
  availability duty. Native code rejected all four as duplicate records. The
  seeded defect itself is not scored as a new live failure; copying it into the
  assessed output is. A warehouse candidate with an empty prefix expanded to
  212 entity records and was also rejected for duplicates. Its other repetition
  was concise but retained joined relationship text. Showing candidate records
  did not make them safe business authority or reliably prevent redundancy.
- **Composition guidance:** candidate admission improved 5/6 to 6/6, but adequate
  resolved composition stayed 1/6 to 1/6. Renewal and warehouse values still
  resolve to `LoanhasReturnDeadline` and `ParcelhasStorageLocation`; one startup
  candidate still resolves consecutive consequences without a separator.
  The FR and AC also vary independently of readability: renewal often replaces
  eligibility with active/not-returned, and the supplied unresolved duration
  disappears from the generated records. The controlled preceding story retains
  that uncertainty, so this is not proof of whole-artifact loss. A warehouse
  response supplies locating behavior while retention is only implied by existing
  data. Extra scenario setup is distinguished from an invented global policy.
- **Evidence ownership:** both received equipment candidates preserve storage
  in their FRs, but their ACs still center on another group's availability duty.
  Canonical relationships remain unreadable. Startup outputs preserve greeting
  bytes and readable behavior, but their token-only assignment remains ambiguous;
  they do not qualify a new rule for restricting contextual content or skipping
  groups. The unsent warehouse candidate pair prevents complete comparison.
- **Source uncertainty:** all twelve requests were unsent. No improvement or
  regression can be inferred for this factor.

Startup success is assessed from stated startup and observable behavior, not a
required occurrence of the word “successfully.” Illustrative equipment/parcel
identifiers are not automatically inventions. Correct greeting bytes in ordinary
string fragments were admitted by the existing owner; these trials did not
exercise typed exact-copy insertion or equal-literal occurrence disambiguation.
Native concatenation added no separators and no rejected response was modified
to make it admissible.

No candidate meets its 6/6 qualification gate. Preserve the useful warehouse

needs a separate candidate that distinguishes supplied candidate context from
assigned authoring duties; do not copy rejected prior output, broaden source
authority, automatically skip ambiguous groups or weaken duplicate validation.

another repetition of that instruction is not an established remedy. Any
representation change must preserve canonical typed text and exact occurrences
through the shared lifecycle. The user chose rollback instead of extending this
experiment. Actual upstream handoff, any future combined-input qualification
and a separately approved published-output E2E remain outstanding.

These are implementation-agent assessments independently checked against sources
and canonical rendered values, not deterministic semantic proof. Development
cases, two repetitions, fixed MOCK predecessors and single-factor inputs do not
establish reliable behavior across models or additive benefits in the combined
production packet.
