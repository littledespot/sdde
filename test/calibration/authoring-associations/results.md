## Consequence

Removing the separate classifier eliminates its independent false-unsupported
decision and the offered-role prebinding gate. This panel establishes that current
authoring can choose structurally valid source associations and that native required
family admission still rejects omissions. It **does not establish reliable authored
meaning or publication**. Fluent output with valid groups still invents conditions,
misattributes an obligation, or omits an applicable family.

The next investigation should focus on the shared record assignment and its native
group/family context, especially why required entity records disappear despite an
explicit decision and why separate-source acceptance criteria select the wrong duty.
Use the retained actual failures and unrelated representatives; do not force a prose
template, remove legitimate groups, relax family admission or infer success from
the absent classifier. Whole-workflow recovery, publication and rubric quality
remain unmeasured until a separately approved E2E run.
