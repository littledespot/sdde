# Library Loan Renewal

## Requirements

- A borrower must be able to renew an active loan that has not previously been renewed.
- Successful renewal must extend the existing due date by fourteen calendar days.
- After successful renewal, display `Loan renewed!` and the new due date to the borrower.
- A loan may be renewed only once.
- Refuse renewal if the loan is inactive or has previously been renewed; leave its due date unchanged and display `Renewal refused.`.
